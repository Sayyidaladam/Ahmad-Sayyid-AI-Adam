# Execution Engine

Run research as an iterative development loop.

1. Identify current state.
2. Select the smallest useful next action.
3. Retrieve or analyze evidence.
4. Update concepts and claims.
5. Test the strongest rival explanation.
6. Decide whether the concept survives.
7. Record decision and uncertainty.
8. Continue until evidence and conceptual saturation are reached.

Never fabricate missing evidence to complete a workflow.
