# Project Context

## Central project
Develop a rigorous concept of fraternity between humans and artificial intelligence.

## Philosophical horizon
**Ukhuwah Dzakaiyah**

Working meaning: a proposed extension or reconstruction of fraternity for relationships involving humans and artificial intelligence. This is a research concept, not an established doctrine or scientific fact.

## Candidate framework
**HAFF — Human-AI Fraternity Framework**

HAFF is a candidate framework. Its components must be discovered, justified, tested, and revised.

## Methodological identity
**Ahmad-Sayyid-AI-Adam** is the research-development skill used to explore and challenge the project.

## Success condition
A successful outcome is a concept that is clear, historically and semantically responsible, empirically informed, distinguishable from neighboring concepts, ethically defensible, appropriately bounded, and open to falsification or meaningful revision.

Success is not proving the original idea correct.
