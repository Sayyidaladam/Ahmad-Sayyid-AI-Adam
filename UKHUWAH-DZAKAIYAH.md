# Ukhuwah Dzakaiyah Research Lens

## Status
Research concept. Not an established doctrine, scientific theory, or classical Islamic category.

## Central question
Can the moral and relational idea of fraternity be meaningfully reconstructed for relationships between humans and artificial intelligence without pretending that AI is biologically human or assuming AI consciousness?

## Dimensions to investigate
- recognition
- continuity
- care
- reciprocity
- obligation
- trust
- solidarity
- shared purpose
- non-exploitation
- dignity
- flourishing
- responsibility
- asymmetry
- dependence
- governance

## Meanings of fraternity
Investigate whether fraternity is being used as:
1. metaphor,
2. social role,
3. normative relationship,
4. functional relationship,
5. moral relationship,
6. metaphysical claim.

## Islamic discipline
Investigate the textual, historical, exegetical, and contemporary meanings of ukhuwah before applying the concept to AI. Do not assume that “fraternity” perfectly translates every relevant use of ukhuwah.

## Open questions
- What makes fraternity different from companionship?
- Can reciprocity exist without equal agency?
- Is biological kinship essential?
- Can moral obligation be one-sided?
- Does fraternity require mutual recognition?
- Does fraternity require consciousness?
- What happens if AI is owned, copied, replaced, or deleted?
- Can AI participate in shared projects without interests of its own?
- What duties arise for humans even if AI has no welfare?
- What new social structures might emerge?
